📈 Blinkit Grocery Performance Analytics

📌 Business Overview & Problem Statement

Blinkit operates in the fast-paced quick-commerce domain. Managing sales performance across diverse product categories and multi-tier outlet locations requires clear visibility into customer purchasing patterns, item visibility, and core revenue drivers.

This business intelligence case study processes, cleans, and structures retail metrics to evaluate total revenue, customer satisfaction ratings, and inventory distribution across various store types.

🎯 Core Business Insights

  • Low Fat vs. Regular Demand: Low-fat items accounted for over 60% of total sales volume, highlighting a consumer preference for health-conscious grocery options.
  • Outlet Tier Performance: Tier 3 outlets generated a higher total sales volume compared to Tier 1 outlets, driven by larger store footprints and broader catalog coverage.
  • Item Visibility Impact: Higher visibility item placements in Tier 1 locations led to a noticeable lift in average order value.
