Predictive Analytics & Retention

📊 Customer Churn Predictive Engine

📌 Business Overview & Problem Statement

Customer retention is a critical driver of profitability for subscription-based businesses, as acquiring new users can cost up to five times more than keeping existing ones.

The goal of this project is to build an end-to-end binary classification model that evaluates client tenure, billing features, and service subscriptions to predict customer churn probability and flag high-risk users for proactive retention campaigns.

⚙️ Data Engineering & Pipeline

  • Target Variable: Churn (Binary: 0 = Retained, 1 = Churned).
  • Feature Transformation: Applied StandardScaler to numerical metrics (Tenure, Monthly Charges) and OneHotEncoder to categorical variables (Contract Type, Payment Method).
  • Pipeline Integration: Combined preprocessing transformers and model architectures inside Scikit-Learn Pipeline objects to prevent data leakage during evaluation.

🤖 Model Evaluation & Selection

Models were evaluated on an 80/20 stratified train-test split:

Model Accuracy Precision Recall F1-Score ROC-AUC
Logistic Regression 78.4% 0.62 0.58 0.60 0.7410
Random Forest Classifier 82.1% 0.71 0.66 0.68 0.8120
XGBoost Classifier (Selected) 85.6% 0.78 0.72 0.75 0.8640

Business Rationale: XGBoost achieved the highest ROC-AUC score (0.8640), allowing customer success teams to identify high-risk churners accurately while minimizing unnecessary marketing spending on low-risk clients.

💡 Key Business Insights

  • Contract Type Impact: Customers on month-to-month plans exhibited a 3x higher churn risk compared to long-term contract holders.
  • Support Ingestion: Clients without tech support subscriptions showed significantly lower retention over a 12-month period.
